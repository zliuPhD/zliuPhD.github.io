About Me

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student in Machine Learning at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Yongxin Chen. Currently, I am also a research intern at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), under the supervision of Dr. Yebin Wang and Dr. Zhaolin Ren. Aside from research, I am a fan of ACGN culture. I also enjoy traveling and swimming.

Research

My research interests span robotics, machine learning, control theory, and stochastic processes. My current research focuses on, but is not limited to, the following topics:

  • Risk-aware planning for robots and vehicles. Develop risk-aware mechanisms for large behavior models, and build scalable safety-assured planning methods for robots and vehicles.
  • Safe control for dynamical systems. For safety-critical and accuracy-critical dynamical systems, develop uncertainty-aware planning and control methods that can provide formal high-probability safety guarantees.
  • Efficiency and real-world effectiveness of control systems. Improve the computational efficiency of learning-based and model-based control methods, and integrate them with hardware-level constraints in real-world settings.
  • Advanced control theory for stochastic systems. Use advanced techniques to analyze reachability, stability, and safety of stochastic systems.
  • Theoretical analysis of diffusion models. Analyze the distributional properties, reachability, and sample efficiency of diffusion models.
